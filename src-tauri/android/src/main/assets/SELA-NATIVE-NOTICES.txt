audio focus, noisy-output pause, notification controls and background queue movement.
SQLite remains owned by Rust via SelaPlugin.nativeStore; there is no second database.
SAF ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT grants persistable read access to local audio.
NewPipe resolves source URLs in-process; Media3 decodes HTTPS/content audio directly.
